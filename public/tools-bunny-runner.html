HOW TO PLAY

Basic Controls

  1. Tap anywhere on the screen to make Bunny jump
  2. Tap and hold to jump higher
  3. Avoid all obstacles - cactus, rocks, and spikes

Scoring System

  1. Pass obstacles: +10 points each
  2. Collect carrots: +10 points each
  3. Golden carrots: +50 points + shield
  4. Jump 3 obstacles without touching ground: 2x combo bonus

Zones & Progression

  1. Every 500 meters = New Zone with different colors
  2. Speed increases slightly each zone
  3. At 1000 meters, a portal appears
  4. Touch the portal to enter Tower Mode

Tower Mode - Vertical Climb

  1. Gravity pulls you down - tap repeatedly to stay up
  2. Avoid walls on left and right sides
  3. Climb as high as possible for massive bonus
  4. Touch exit portal at top to return with rewards

Power-Ups

  1. Golden Carrot: Gives you a shield for one hit
  2. Star: Makes you invincible for 5 seconds
  3. Collect carrots in a row for combo multiplier

Tips

  1. Time your jumps carefully in later zones
  2. Risk vs Reward: Best carrots are near obstacles
  3. Use portals to escape when the run gets too fast
  4. Practice in Zone 1 to master the timing
